# Chapter 6: Equipment Supplier Duopolies & Pricing Power Dynamics

## 6.1 The WFE Oligopoly
The tools inside a GigaFab are not commodities; they are proprietary technologies supplied by five global companies:
1. **ASML:** $100\%$ monopoly in EUV lithography.
2. **Lam Research:** $\approx 45\%$ monopoly in conductor and dielectric dry etch.
3. **Applied Materials (AMAT):** Market share leader in PVD, CMP, and epitaxy.
4. **Tokyo Electron (TEL):** Leader in photoresist track coating/development and cryogenic etch.
5. **KLA Corporation:** $\approx 50\%$ market share in optical inspection and overlay metrology.

## 6.2 Structural Pricing Power
Because a fab cannot substitute an alternative vendor without halting production for months, equipment vendors command immense pricing power:
- **EUV Scanner ASP:** Grew from $\$100\text{M}$ (0.33 NA) to **$\$350\text{M}–\$380\text{M}$ for High-NA (0.55 NA)**.
- **Gross Margins:** Equipment vendors reliably capture **$45\%–55\%$ gross margins**.
- **Service Monopoly:** Fabs must sign proprietary service contracts, guaranteeing equipment vendors a multi-billion-dollar recurring annuity.
