# Chapter 1: The Anatomy of a $20 Billion GigaFab: Capex Breakdown

## 1.1 Capital Allocation Breakdown of an Advanced Fab
Constructing and outfitting a modern 3nm/2nm GigaFab (capacity: $50,000\text{ to } 100,000\text{ wafer starts/month}$) requires **$18\text{B to } $24\text{B}** in upfront capital expenditure:
- **Wafer Fabrication Equipment (WFE): 75% to 80% ($14B – $18B)**
  - Lithography (ASML High-NA & EUV): $30\%–35\%$ of total WFE
  - Etch & Cleans (Lam, TEL, AMAT): $22\%–26\%$
  - Deposition & ALD (AMAT, Lam, ASMI): $18\%–22\%$
  - Metrology & Inspection (KLA, AMAT): $10\%–12\%$
  - Ion Implant & CMP (Axcelis, AMAT, Ebara): $6\%–8\%$
- **Cleanroom & Building Facility: 15% ($2.5B – $3.5B)**
  - Ballroom Class 1 cleanroom ($100,000\text{ m}^2$)
  - Anti-vibration waffle slabs isolated from external seismic tremors
  - Massive sub-fab basement housing thousands of dry vacuum pumps, abatement scrubbers, and RF generators
- **Ultra-Pure Utilities & Piping: 5% ($1.0B – $1.5B)**
  - Ultra-Pure Water (UPW) facility recycling millions of gallons daily with parts-per-trillion ionic purity
  - Bulk specialty gas piping (toxic hydrides, halogens, ultra-pure $\text{N}_2, \text{Ar}, \text{He}$)
  - Dual-grid high-voltage electrical substations with uninterruptible power supplies (UPS).
