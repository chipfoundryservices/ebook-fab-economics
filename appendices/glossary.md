# Appendix A: Fab Economics Glossary

- **AMHS (Automated Material Handling System):** Robotic cleanroom overhead rail system transporting wafer FOUPs between tool bays.
- **ASP (Average Selling Price):** Mean revenue received per manufactured $300	ext{mm}$ wafer.
- **Capacity Utilization:** Percentage of a fab's maximum theoretical wafer throughput currently being processed.
- **Capex (Capital Expenditure):** Funds invested to construct cleanroom facilities and acquire wafer fab equipment.
- **Cleanroom Ballroom:** Expansive Class 1 cleanroom space where process tools are installed on a raised anti-vibration floor.
- **Depreciation:** Systematic accounting allocation of an equipment asset's initial cost over its useful accounting life (typically 5 years).
- **Die Yield ($Y$):** Percentage of total fabricated chips on a wafer that meet all operational specifications.
- **FOUP (Front Opening Unified Pod):** Sealed plastic enclosure carrying 25 wafers in an ultra-clean micro-environment.
- **GigaFab:** Megascale semiconductor manufacturing facility producing $>100,000$ $300	ext{mm}$ wafer starts per month.
- **Operational Leverage:** Degree to which a percentage change in wafer volume translates into a larger percentage change in operating income.
- **ROIC (Return on Invested Capital):** Core profitability metric: Net Operating Profit After Tax (NOPAT) divided by Total Invested Capital.
- **WFE (Wafer Fabrication Equipment):** High-precision capital machinery used to fabricate semiconductor devices.
