# Book #13: Fab Economics & Strategic Capital Allocation
## Equipment Depreciation, Service Revenue Moats, and the Long-Term Value Creation Model of Semiconductor Manufacturing

**Book #13 in the ChipFoundryServices Technical Series (Capstone)**

---

## Overview

*Fab Economics & Strategic Capital Allocation* is the capstone book synthesizing all technical knowledge (Books #1-12) into the financial and strategic frameworks that determine semiconductor industry winners and losers.

This book answers the fundamental question: Why do companies like TSMC, Samsung, and Intel spend $20B+ annually on fab construction, while smaller fabs cannot compete?

Key topics:
- **Capex economics** (equipment depreciation, fab productivity per dollar invested, ROI timelines)
- **Process technology transitions** (cost of node shrinks, yield learning curves, competitive racing)
- **Service revenue moats** (maintenance, spares, process support, customer lock-in)
- **Supply chain strategy** (single-supplier dependencies, vertical integration, resilience)
- **Capital allocation decisions** (technology roadmap investments, merger economics, shareholder value)
